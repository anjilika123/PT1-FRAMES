Midnight Snack

1. Garlic Cheese Toast

Ingredients: Bread slices, Butter or margarine, minced garlic, Cheese

Steps:

  1. Mix the softened butter and minced garlic in a small bowl.
  2. Spread the garlic butter evenly on one side of each bread slice.
  3. Place cheese on the unbuttered side of one slice, then cover with another slice, buttered side facing out.
  4. Heat a pan over low to medium heat.
  5. Toast the sandwich in the pan until the bread is golden brown.
  6. Flip and toast the other side until the cheese melts.
  7. Remove from the pan, slice if desired, and eat while hot.
