Dessert

1. Mango Graham

Ingredients: 3–4 ripe mangoes, 2 packs graham crackers, 2 cups all-purpose cream, 1 can condensed milk, extra crushed graham crackers, vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Prepare the cream mixture: Melt the butter and set it aside to cool slightly.
  2. Prepare the mangoes: Peel the mangoes and slice them thinly.
  3. Assemble the first layer: Arrange a single layer of graham crackers at the bottom of a dish.
  4. Add cream and mangoes: Spread a layer of the cream mixture over the crackers. Add a layer of sliced mangoes on top.
  5. Repeat the layers:Continue layering graham crackers, cream, and mangoes until the dish is filled.
  6. Finish the top layer:End with cream on top, then add mango slices or crushed grahams.
  7. Chill:Cover and refrigerate for at least 4–6 hours or overnight for best texture.
